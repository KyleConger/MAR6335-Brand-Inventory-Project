--- SLIDE 12 ---
Projective Techniques: Pros And Cons
Pros
Circumvents unwilling/unable to respond
Adds “richness” to findings
Discovery of unanticipated ideas
Cons
Exploratory only
No evidence of reliability and validity
Difficult to use for large samples
