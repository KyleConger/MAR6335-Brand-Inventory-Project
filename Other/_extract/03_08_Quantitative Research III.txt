--- SLIDE 14 ---
Net Promoter Score (NPS)
"On a scale of zero to 10, how likely is it that you would recommend Bounty to your friends or colleagues?" *
	Detractors (D) = 0 through 6
	Promoters (P) = 9 or 10
	NPS (%) = %P - %D
*Since NPS is a measure of brand loyalty, analyze responses only from those who use the product!

--- SLIDE 15 ---
Conclusion
As you conduct your brand audit, be sure to include measures of each of the components of brand equity.
Wherever possible, include perceptions of competing brands to get a more complete picture.
