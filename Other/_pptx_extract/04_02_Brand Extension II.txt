Beyond Fit: Extension Authenticity Brand Extension Authenticity (BEA) refers to the consumer’s sense that a brand extension is a legitimate, culturally consistent extension of the parent brand. BEA is comprised of four inter-related dimensions that can be assessed for any existing or proposed brand extension by asking consumers to respond to a series of rating scales.

--- SLIDE 12 ---
Beyond Fit: Extension Authenticity (2) Maintaining brand style and standards (“This extension appears to be inferior to ______.”) Honoring brand heritage (“______ seems to have abandoned it roots with this extension.”) Preserving brand essence (“What makes ______ distinct to me is lost in this extension.”) Avoiding brand exploitation (“This extension likely trades off the essence of ______ strictly for profit.”)

--- SLIDE 14 ---
Other Findings… High quality parent brands extend “further” than average quality parent brands. More “abstract” parent brand associations are easier to extend than are “concrete” associations. A successful extension creates the potential for even further extensions of the parent brand. An unsuccessful extension is most damaging when there is a strong fit between the parent and the extension.

--- SLIDE 15 ---
More Findings… Extensions are most successful when the parent brand enjoys a high degree of brand loyalty. Vertical extensions are difficult and may require sub-branding. Strong marketing support at launch is critical to an extension’s success. Retailer acceptance is critical to extension success.

--- SLIDE 16 ---
The Brand Extension Decision Process Fully research parent brand equity (esp. associations) Determine extension category( ies ) potential Evaluate transferability and relevance of brand assets to extension category Assessment of superiority of extension to competition Assessment of company resources to sustain competition Develop and execute launch strategy Evaluate extension success and feedback effect on parent
