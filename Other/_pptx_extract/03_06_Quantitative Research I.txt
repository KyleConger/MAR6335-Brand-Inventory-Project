Example of Telescoping QUESTION A: Several products are listed below. Please indicate with an “X” each product that you or anyone in your household has purchased in the last three months. QUESTION B: Several products are listed below. Please indicate with an “X” which products you or anyone in your household has ever purchased. Then, for each product ever bought, please indicate with an “X” which products have been purchased in the last three months.

--- SLIDE 10 ---
Close-Ended Questions Answers should be… Mutually exclusive No overlapping categories e.g., “never used; used once; used twice; used 3 or more times” e.g., “Age: 0-10; older than 10 but younger than 20; 20 or older but younger than 30; 30 – 50; older than 50” Collectively exhaustive That is, account for all possible answers To be safe, use: __ Other (explain) ______________________ Use words respondents use Determined through exploratory research

Nominal Measurement Nominal – numbers are used only to classify Which of the following candy bars do you like? (Check all that apply) _____Twix _____Mars _____Snickers _____Milky Way

--- SLIDE 13 ---
Ordinal Scale Ordinal – numbers used to indicate rank Please rank the candy bars on the following list according to your liking for each, assigning 1 to your most preferred and 4 to your least preferred. _____Twix _____Mars _____Snickers _____Milky Way
