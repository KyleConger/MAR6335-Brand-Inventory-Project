--- SLIDE 14 ---
Observation: Pros Major Advantages: Ability to observe actual marketplace behavior. People do not always remember what they actually do. People sometimes misrepresent what they do. “Discovery” of unanticipated phenomena

--- SLIDE 15 ---
Observation: Cons Major Disadvantages: Depends on researcher’s interpretations. Without inquiry, researcher must infer motives Must wait for target behavior to happen Risk of idle time
