--- SLIDE 3 ---
Brand Portfolio Strategy Brand Portfolio Strategy is the design, deployment and management of multiple brands as a coordinated portfolio of meaning-based assets that address the needs of diverse sets of customers in the marketplace, maximizing return while minimizing risk. The brand portfolio is a complete cataloging of the organization’s brands and their variants. Brand architecture is the blueprint of the interrelationships among the various brands in the portfolio, specifying their respective scope and role.

--- SLIDE 8 ---
Endorsed Brands Benefits Authentication by parent brand More differentiation than sub-brand Stretch the master brand farther Risks Less positive spillover from parent brand Requires more promotional resources Less potential for positive feedback effects

--- SLIDE 9 ---
House of Brands Independent brand identities, no visible endorsement

--- SLIDE 10 ---
House of Brands Pros and Cons Benefits Easier differentiation and targeting of segments Captures greater retail shelf space Capitalizes on variety-seeking customers Risks Cannibalization Higher marketing and operational costs Limits brand expansion opportunities

--- SLIDE 11 ---
Co-Brands Benefits Share brand equity between two independent brands Novelty can attract attention Work best when a logical “fit” exists between the brands Risks Financial partnership conflicts Increases exposure to marketplace failures
