--- SLIDE 4 ---
Question Content Is the question necessary? How will it be used to address the research purpose? Has the question been adequately covered by other questions? Do respondents have the necessary information? Will respondents provide the information? Avoid embarrassing or threatening questions Address sensitive issues later in the survey

--- SLIDE 5 ---
Guidelines for Question Wording Use simple words and language Have you ever experienced cognitive dissonance after buying a new car? Avoid ambiguity What is the most important consideration in buying a car? Avoid leading questions Many consumers are concerned about the security of Internet transactions. What is your chief concern about using the Internet to make purchases?

--- SLIDE 6 ---
More Question Guidelines Avoid implicit assumptions How can Starbucks improve its excellent customer service? Avoid generalizations How do you feel about recent movies? Avoid double-barreled questions How would you rate the friendliness and knowledge of Best Buy salespeople?

--- SLIDE 7 ---
Guidelines for Sequencing Use simple, interesting opening questions to build momentum Use the funnel approach, asking broad questions first Group questions by topic or form Place demographics, as well as difficult or sensitive questions, toward the end Be wary of context effects

Conclusion Advantage of surveys is the numerical data they generate—use a preponderance of scaled measures At least some of the measures should be relative to the competition Questionnaire should be designed with input from qualitative research Pretest the questionnaire to be sure it works
