Branding Origins “Branding” has been around for centuries. Origin of the term in the Old Norse word, brandr , which means “to burn.”

--- SLIDE 4 ---
Official Definition of “Brand” “...a name, term, sign, symbol, design, or combination…intended to IDENTIFY the goods or services of one seller…and to differentiate them from the competition.”

--- SLIDE 5 ---
User-Friendly Definition A brand is a source of a promise of relevantly differentiated benefits.

--- SLIDE 6 ---
What is a Brand – Really? A distinguishing name, mark, or symbol A signature indicating source or origin A signal of quality A badge of customer identification A “virtual contract” between consumer/brand
