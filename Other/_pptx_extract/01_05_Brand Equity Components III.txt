Perceived Quality Definition: A principal dimension of a brand’s positioning, perceived quality is the customer’s perception of the overall quality of the brand with respect to its intended purpose, relative to competitors.

--- SLIDE 4 ---
Perceived Quality: “Moments of Truth” “Each of SAS’ 10 million annual customers comes into contact with an average of five SAS employees on each flight, for approximately 15 seconds each... These 50 million ‘moments of truth’ ultimately determine whether SAS will succeed or fail as a company. These are the moments when we must prove to our customers SAS is their ‘best’ alternative.” - Jan Carlzon, CEO, Scandinavian Airlines (SAS)
