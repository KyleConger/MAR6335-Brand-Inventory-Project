Brand Architecture & Hierarchy Brand architecture is the blueprint of the interrelationships among the various brands in the portfolio, specifying their respective scopes and roles. Brand hierarchy is an explicit ordering of the company’s brands along some relevant dimension (e.g., price)

--- SLIDE 4 ---
Designing the Brand Architecture Requires “systems thinking” about: Brand scope Key brand roles Relationships among brands
