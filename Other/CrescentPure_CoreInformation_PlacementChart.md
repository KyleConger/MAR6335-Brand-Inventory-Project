## Use guidance

- Keep case facts, analysis, and recommendation visibly separate.
- Cite the case page and the exact lecture deck/slide wherever a course concept drives the analysis.
- Treat “organic refreshment” as a possible broad positioning route whose appeal and execution feasibility must be tested.
- Treat PDB-to-Crescent fit primarily as corporate portfolio and capability logic unless additional evidence establishes a consumer-facing parent-brand relationship.
