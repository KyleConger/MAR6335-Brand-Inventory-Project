## Survey instrument

**Title:** Athletic Footwear Brand Perceptions Study  

**Intro:** University of Florida graduate marketing class project. Anonymous. No right/wrong answers. ~8–12 minutes.

### Section 0 — Consent

**Q0.** Agree to participate? ☐ Yes ☐ No → End

### Section 1 — Screening / category use

**Q1.** Past 12 months: purchased or seriously considered athletic/running shoes? ☐ Yes ☐ No  

**Q2.** How often do you run/cardio in athletic shoes?  
☐ Never ☐ Rarely ☐ Occasionally (1–3×/month) ☐ Regularly (1–2×/week) ☐ Frequently (3+×/week)  
*(Categories non-overlapping — `03_06`.)*

**Q3.** Primary use *(one)*: ☐ Running/training ☐ Gym ☐ Walking/everyday ☐ Sports ☐ Fashion/lifestyle ☐ Other: ___

### Section 2 — Awareness (`01_03` pyramid; `03_08` measures)

**Q4. Unaided recall.** List athletic/running shoe brands you can think of (up to 8 open boxes).  
*Code: top-of-mind = first mention; any unaided On mention.*

**Q5. Consideration set.** Brands you would consider purchasing next (open or multi-select after coding).

**Q6. Aided recall.** Heard of? *(select all)* Nike · Hoka · Brooks · Asics · New Balance · Adidas · Saucony · **On (On Cloud / On Running)** · Altra · Other ___ · None

**Q7. Recognition** *(On logo on this page only).* Seen this brand mark before? ☐ Yes ☐ No ☐ Not sure

**Q8.** Familiarity with On: Not at all familiar **1 2 3 4 5** Extremely familiar *(interval — `03_06`)*

### Section 3 — Brand image associations (identity match + emergent)

Brief cue: “On (On Cloud / On Running) is a Swiss athletic footwear brand.”  
Offer ☐ No opinion / Don’t know (treat as missing for means).

For **each** A1–A5 and E1–E4, ask Strength, Valence, Uniqueness on the **exact** 1–5 assignment scales above.

**A1** Soft / cushioned “cloud” ride — S / V / U  
**A2** Lightweight, energetic performance — S / V / U  
**A3** Swiss-engineered technical innovation — S / V / U  
**A4** Stylish / design-forward athletic aesthetic — S / V / U  
**A5** Premium / elevated running lifestyle — S / V / U  

**E1** Durability / longevity — S / V / U  
**E2** Fit / sizing / narrow — S / V / U  
**E3** Everyday / lifestyle wear — S / V / U  
**E4** Premium / expensive / price — S / V / U  

**Optional Q9.** Rank importance of finalized associations when choosing running shoes (ordinal — `03_06`).

### Section 4 — Perceived quality vs. competitors (`01_05`; `03_08`)

**Q10.** Overall quality of each brand: Very low **1** … **5** Very high · ☐ No opinion  

| Brand | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | No opinion |

| Asics | | | | | | |

**Q11.** Relative to other running brands you know, On’s quality is: Much worse **1 2 3 4 5** Much better · ☐ No basis  

**Q12.** On attribute quality (1=Poor … 5=Excellent · ☐ No opinion): Cushioning/comfort · Durability · Fit · Style · Value for money · Running performance  
*(Avoid double-barreled single items — `03_07`; each attribute separate.)*

### Section 5 — Loyalty (`01_06`; `03_08`)

**Q13.** Own On shoes? ☐ Yes ☐ No ☐ Used to, but not now  

**Q14.** Satisfaction with On *(if ever owned)*: Very dissatisfied **1 2 3 4 5** Very satisfied  

**Q15.** Likelihood of **repurchase**: Very unlikely **1 2 3 4 5** Very likely  

**Q16. NPS** (`01_06` / `03_08`): Recommend On to friend/colleague? **0**–**10**  
*Users only for NPS: Promoters 9–10; Passives 7–8; Detractors 0–6; NPS = %P − %D.*

**Q17. Preference** (ordinal rank 1=most preferred): On · Hoka · Nike · Brooks · Asics · New Balance  

**Q18.** Loyalty self-placement (nominal):  
☐ On is my preferred brand ☐ One of several I like ☐ Aware but prefer others ☐ Not really aware ☐ Actively avoid On  
*(Rough map to loyalty pyramid language in `01_06` for interpretation — not a claim that the pyramid is a validated survey scale.)*

### Section 6 — Brand personality (`02_03`; `03_08`)

**Q19. Checklist** (select all — nominal):  
Sincerity: Down-to-earth · Honest · Wholesome · Cheerful  
Excitement: Daring · Spirited · Imaginative · Up-to-date  
Competence: Reliable · Intelligent · Successful  
Sophistication: Upper-class · Charming  
Ruggedness: Outdoorsy · Tough  
Category adds: Trendy · Technical · Minimalist · Premium · ☐ None / Don’t know  

**Q20. Semantic differentials** (interval 1–5): Traditional–Innovative · Mass-market–Premium · Utilitarian–Stylish · Performance–Lifestyle/fashion · Ordinary–Distinctive  

### Section 7 — Demographics (end — `03_07`)

**Q21.** Age bands **Q22.** Gender **Q23.** Student status **Q24.** Approx. athletic footwear spend
