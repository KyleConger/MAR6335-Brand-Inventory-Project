# On Cloud — Moderator Guide Timing (45–60 min Depth Interview)

**Pairs with:** `OnCloud_Qualitative_Interview_Protocol.md`  
**Course method:** Individual depth interviews; funnel; free elicitation; optional projective/sentence completion (`03_02_Qualitative Research II.pptx`, `03_03_Qualitative Research III.pptx`).  
**Sample rule:** n≥10; Zoom or face-to-face.

---

## Timing overview

| Block | Minutes | Focus | Course hook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Welcome / consent / recording | 3–4 | Rapport; purpose; anonymity | Ethics / logistics |
| 1. Category warm-up | 5–6 | When/why buy athletic shoes; consideration language | Consideration sets (`02_02`) |
| 2. Unaided brand landscape | 5–7 | Brands that come to mind; order of mention | Awareness pyramid (`01_03`); free elicitation (`03_03`) |
| 3. Free associations — On | 8–10 | “First thing you think of when I say On / On Cloud?”; meaning of obvious | Associations / image (`01_04`) |
| 4. Personality & user imagery | 5–7 | If On were a person; “people who wear On…” | Brand personality (`02_03`) |
| 5. Quality vs competitors | 7–8 | Rate/compare On vs 2–3 brands they know on cushioning, durability, value, run quality | Perceived quality relative (`01_05`) |
| 6. Loyalty / repertoire | 5–6 | Own/switch/recommend; rotation behavior | Loyalty pyramid (`01_06`) |
| 7. Identity probes (late only) | 4–5 | Soft probes on provisional A1–A5 **if not emerged** | Gap prep — do not lead early |
| 8. Close | 2–3 | Thanks; incentive; ask for survey referral | |

**45-min version:** shorten blocks 4 and 7. **60-min version:** add laddering (“why is that important?”) on 1–2 associations (`03_02` chain of questions).

---

## Must-ask prompts (keep wording open)

- Top of mind: “What is the first thing you think of when I say ‘On’ or ‘On Cloud’?” (`03_02`)  
- Meaning: “What does that mean to you?”  
- Sentence completion: “The best thing about On is…” / “People who wear On Clouds are…”  
- Purpose split (from C2C): “Are On shoes more for running, walking/work, or both? Why?”  
- Competitor: “If you couldn’t buy On, what would you buy instead—and why?”

---

## Moderator don’ts

- Do not read the Brand Identity Matrix early.  
- Do not defend the brand.  
- Do not treat C2C stereotypes as facts to confirm—probe whether *this* respondent holds them.  
- Record emergent associations for survey E-slots (durability, fit, lifestyle crossover, value).
