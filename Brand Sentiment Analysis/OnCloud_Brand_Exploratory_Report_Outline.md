## Part I — Brand Exploratory (~2000 words)

### I.1 Introduction & methods overview (~150–200)

**Prompts**

- Brand audited: On Cloud / On Running (`Choices/Overall Decisions.md`)  
- Purpose: understand consumer **image** (vs company **identity** from inventory)  
- Three required studies: (a) C2C web search (b) qualitative n≥10 Zoom/F2F (c) survey n≥100  
- State sample sizes, recruitment, and that means/frequencies suffice (no stats tests required)  
- Note instruments: protocol + questionnaire in attachments; C2C Excel workbook  

**Course hook:** Qual for discovery & questionnaire input (`03_01`); Quant for scaled CBBE measures (`03_08`).

### I.2 C2C web search results (~450–550)

**Prompts**

- Platforms mined (Reddit JSONL → `OnCloud_C2C_Brand_Exploratory.xlsx`; plus any Amazon/social if added)  
- Focus on **meaning**, not star ratings (assignment)  
- Text mining / common associations (`03_01_Qualitative Research I`)  
- Evidence bullets: dominant associations, valence, competitor contrasts, quality/loyalty talk  
- List **emergent** associations (candidates for E1–E4) not in the five identity associations  
- Clear labeling: evidence vs interpretation  

**Exhibit ideas:** word/theme frequency table; sample verbatim quotes; competitor co-mention chart  

### I.3 Qualitative research (~500–600)

**Prompts**

- Method: depth interviews; funnel; free elicitation; optional laddering/projectives (`03_02`–`03_05`)  
- n≥10; Zoom/F2F logistics; who was recruited  
- Results by CBBE lens:  
  - Awareness (TOM/recall/recognition language from `01_03`)  
  - Associations / personality (`01_04`, `02_03`)  
  - Perceived quality vs competitors (`01_05`)  
  - Loyalty / advocacy (`01_06`)  
- Emergent themes → how they shaped survey wording  
- Do **not** pretend interview counts are population stats (`03_02` analysis caution)

**Exhibit ideas:** theme map; illustrative quotes; personality adjective summary  

### I.4 Survey results (~650–750)

**Prompts**

- n≥100; Qualtrics; recruitment; pretest note (`03_07`)  
- **Awareness:** % unaided / aided / recognition; familiarity mean (`01_03`, `03_08`)  
- **Associations:** for each of 5 identity associations (+ emergent): mean Strength, Valence, Uniqueness → fill Brand Image Matrix  
- **Perceived quality:** On vs Hoka/Nike/Brooks/Asics/New Balance means (`01_05`)  
- **Loyalty:** repurchase, preference ranks, NPS among users (`01_06`, `03_08`)  
- **Personality:** checklist frequencies; semantic differential means (`02_03`, `03_08`)  
- Separate **evidence** (tables) from **analysis** (save deep gap talk for Part II)  
- **Never invent numbers** — placeholders until data collected  

**Exhibit ideas:** awareness funnel chart; association means table; quality comparison bars; NPS breakdown; personality profile

## Part II — Brand Analysis and Strategy (~500 words)

### II.1 Analysis — Inventory vs Exploratory / Identity–Image gaps (~300)

**Prompts**

- Assess four CBBE components from survey (+ qual/C2C color)  
- Compare company view (Brand Identity Matrix from inventory) vs consumer Brand Image Matrix  
- Side-by-side deltas (use gap workbook sheets)  
- Where identity is strong but image weak (opportunity to build associations)  
- Where image includes emergent associations firm may underweight  
- Opportunities to enhance equity via association strength / favorability / uniqueness (`01_04` value of associations)

**Required:** Brand Image Matrix exhibit (excluded from 500-word & 10-exhibit caps)

### II.2 Strategy implications (~200)

**Prompts — at least 1, preferably 2–3 actionable recommendations** tied directly to gaps:

1. Sustain / strengthen high-equity associations (e.g., if cloud cushioning is intended POD but uniqueness mean is low → communication/proof)  
2. Close quality or durability perception gaps if C2C/survey show them  
3. Loyalty/advocacy play (NPS detractors; switching triggers)  
4. Personality/positioning consistency (`02_03`) if image drifts (e.g., fashion vs performance)  

Use `OnCloud_Brand_Image_Identity_Gap_Template.xlsx` → **Strategy_Implications** sheet as drafting space.
