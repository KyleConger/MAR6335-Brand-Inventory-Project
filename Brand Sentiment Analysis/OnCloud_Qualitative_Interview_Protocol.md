## 1. Purpose

Discover how consumers **perceive** On (brand **image**), as distinct from what the firm intends (brand **identity** from the Brand Inventory). Primary dimensions match the brand exploratory assignment and CBBE slides:

1. **Awareness** (recognition and/or recall)  
2. **Associations** / brand meaning  
3. **Perceived quality** relative to competitors  
4. **Loyalty** (including advocacy)  
5. **Brand personality** (human characteristics)

Qualitative work here is for **discovery** and to **feed questionnaire design** (`03_01_Qualitative Research I`), not for population percentages.

## 5. Interview flow (funnel)

### A. Warm-up (build rapport; simple opening)

1. How do athletic / running shoes fit into your week?  
2. What activities do you use them for?  
3. Last purchase: what mattered most?

### B. Category → competitive set (awareness context)

4. When I say “running shoes,” what first comes to mind?  
5. First brand that comes to mind? Other brands? *(start of recall / consideration-set language from `01_03` and `03_08`)*  
6. Which brands would you seriously consider next? Why?

*Do not mention On unless the respondent does.*

### C. Awareness: unaided recall → recognition (`01_03` pyramid; `03_08` measures)

7. Have you heard of **On / On Cloud / On Running**?  
8. Unaided: “What is the first thing you think of when I say On / On Cloud?”  
9. **Only after unaided:** show logo / silhouette — recognition probe.

### D. Free associations & meaning (`01_04`; `03_03` free elicitation)

10. What qualities/attributes do you associate with On? (“What else?”)  
11. Tag silently: attribute, benefit, Swiss/country, price, lifestyle, user, competitor contrast.  
12. Who typically wears On? Who would not?  
13. Feelings linked to the brand?  
14. **Brand personality** (`02_03_Brand Positioning III`; `03_08`): If On were a person, describe them. Optional: more *sincere / exciting / competent / sophisticated / rugged*?  
15. Optional **ladder** (`03_04`): pick one strong association → keep asking “Why is that important to you?”

### E. Perceived quality vs. competitors (`01_05`)

16. How does On’s overall quality compare with Hoka, Nike, Brooks, Asics, New Balance (as known)?  
17. On which dimensions (cushioning, durability, fit, style, value, run performance) does it win or lose?  
18. Any “moments of truth” (try-on, first run, after 100 miles) that shaped quality perception?

### F. Loyalty / switching / advocacy (`01_06`; NPS recommend language)

19. Own On? Buy again?  
20. Where on a loyalty continuum: indifferent / habitual / satisfied with switching costs / “likes the brand” / committed?  
21. Recommend to a friend who runs? What would you say?  
22. What would make you switch away—or try On for the first time?

### G. Optional projective / sentence completion (`03_02`, `03_05`)

23. “The best thing about On is…” / “The worst thing about On is…”  
24. “People who wear On are…”  
25. “If On were a car / city / athlete, it would be… Why?”  
26. “Compared with Hoka / Nike / Brooks, On is more ___ and less ___.”

### H. Closing

27. Anything we missed about how you see On?  
28. Thanks; incentive; optional survey pilot follow-up.
