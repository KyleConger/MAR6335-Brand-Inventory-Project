# II. Analysis & Strategy (~500 words)

*Adapted from `OnCloud_Brand_Analysis_Strategy_Draft.md`. Trim further for final Word count.*

## Analysis — company identity vs consumer image

**Brand identity** (firm-intended) vs **brand image** (consumer-salient) drives the gap exhibit. Ogilvy’s consumer *idea* of the product and co-authored brand culture (`01_01`) imply gaps are expected — but strategy requires matching lists (`04` assignment).

| Component | Identity (provisional / `[IDENTITY MATRIX PENDING]`) | Image (C2C Evidence) | Gap read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness (`01_03`) | Seek recognition & recall in running + lifestyle | Strong community proxies; population TOM unknown | Survey unaided/aided/recognition; do not treat Reddit volume as % |
| Associations (`01_04`) | Soft cloud ride; Swiss tech; style; premium lifestyle *(provisional)* | Style 1,654; fit 1,092; comfort 889; lifestyle 882; durability 690; Swiss 209; CloudTec uniqueness 59 | Lifestyle/style may outrun Swiss/tech; E1/E2 are risk themes |
| Perceived quality (`01_05`) | Premium engineered running quality | Comfort/aesthetics praised; fit & durability contested; purpose split by model | Relative PQ vs Asics/Nike/Hoka required |
| Loyalty (`01_06`) | Aspire committed recommenders | Advocacy 322 vs criticism 290; mid-pyramid repertoire | NPS among users only |

**POD/POP (`02_01`):** Potential POD = CloudTec/design distinctiveness; risk that lifestyle POP crowds running PODs vs Hoka cushion or Nike systems. Positioning hierarchy: talk often stuck at features/use (pods, workwear) rather than higher-order values. Perceptual sketch (`02_02`) places On toward lifestyle + comfort pending survey validation.

## Strategy recommendations (2–3)

1. **Clarify master-brand job-to-be-done across the line.** *(C2C-backed direction; execution speculative until qual/survey)* Lead performance proof on hero runners; fence Cloud 5/6 lifestyle SKUs with explicit everyday/commute cues so consideration sets form correctly (`02_02`).  
2. **Close durability and fit as POPs; protect design/CloudTec as POD if uniqueness holds.** *(C2C-backed)* Relative quality (`01_05`) requires comparative survey ratings before locking spend.  
3. **Build loyalty at the model-adopter tier, not awareness alone.** *(Partly C2C-backed)* NPS among On users (`03_08`); address detractors on durability/value; do not equate Cloud 5 visibility with committed loyalty (`01_06`).
