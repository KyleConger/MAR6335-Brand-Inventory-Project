## 3. Screener (qual + optional survey gate)

Use as reply form or Qualtrics block 0. Goal: athletic/running shoe buyers or serious considerers; mix of On owners, aware non-buyers, and some unaware (`OnCloud_Qualitative_Interview_Protocol.md`).

1. In the past 12 months, have you **purchased** or **seriously considered** athletic/running shoes?  
   - Yes / No → if No, thank & end (or keep only a few unaware for recognition probes)
2. Which best describes you today?  
   - Own ≥1 pair of On / On Cloud / On Running  
   - Aware of On but do not own  
   - Not sure / not familiar with On  
3. Which other brands are in your consideration set? (multi-select)  
   - Nike · Hoka · Asics · Brooks · Adidas · New Balance · Saucony · Other: ___
4. Primary use of athletic shoes? (multi OK)  
   - Running training/racing · Gym/cross-training · Everyday/lifestyle · Walking/work · Other: ___
5. Age band (or “prefer not to say”) + consent to Zoom recording (qual only): Yes / No  

**Recruit extras:** aim for ~12 scheduled Zoom slots to land ≥10 completes (`03_02` no-show buffer).
