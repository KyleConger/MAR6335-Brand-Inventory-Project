## Analysis — Company identity vs consumer image

Course distinction (`Assignments/02-brand-inventory-report.md`; echoed in exploratory assignment): **brand identity** = associations the firm intends; **brand image** = associations salient to consumers. Ogilvy’s line in `01_01_What is a Brand.pptx` — a brand is the consumer’s *idea* of a product — and the “Authors of Brand Culture” slide remind us customers co-author meaning; gaps are expected.

| Equity component | Company identity (provisional / inventory TBD) | Consumer image (C2C evidence) | Gap read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Awareness** (`01_03` pyramid) | Likely seek strong recognition & recall in running + lifestyle | Strong community/proxy presence (r/OnRunning; Cloud* naming); population TOM vs Nike/Hoka/Asics **unknown** | Measure unaided/aided/recognition (`03_08`); do not treat Reddit volume as awareness % |
| **Associations** (`01_04`) | Soft cloud ride; Swiss tech; style; premium performance lifestyle *(provisional)* | Excel top image themes: style (1,654), fit/narrow (1,092), comfort (889), lifestyle (882), durability (690); Swiss (209) & CloudTec uniqueness (59) much lower | Lifestyle/style may outrun Swiss/tech identity; fit & durability are emergent risks |
| **Perceived quality** (`01_05`) | Premium engineered running quality | Aesthetics/comfort/cushioning often praised; fit & durability contested; purpose split Cloud 5/6 vs Cloudmonster/Cloudboom | Relative PQ vs Asics/Nike/Hoka must be surveyed; purpose confusion dilutes “intended purpose” |
| **Loyalty** (`01_06` pyramid) | Aspire committed / recommending buyers | Advocacy 322 vs criticism 290; repurchase 135; switching-away 132; committed-love 124 | Mid-pyramid / repertoire brand; NPS among users only |

**Personality / authenticity** (`02_03`): Excel cue map skews Excitement (271) + Sophistication (244); Competence (161) secondary; Sincerity thin (68). Authenticity risk if “running brand” identity meets “suburbia lifestyle shoe” image.

**POD/POP** (`02_01`): Potential POD = distinctive CloudTec / design signal; risk that lifestyle POP with fashion sneakers crowds out running PODs vs Hoka maximal cushion or Nike racing systems. Positioning hierarchy (`02_01`): consumers often stuck at **features/use** (pods, workwear) rather than owned higher-order values.

## Strategy recommendations (actionable)

1. **Clarify master-brand job-to-be-done across the line architecture.** *(Evidence-backed direction; execution details speculative until survey)*  
   C2C shows Cloud 5/6 coded as walk/work and Cloudmonster/Cloudboom as running. Use brand engineering levers from `01_01` (product design, retail, messaging) so consideration sets (`02_02`) form around the correct model. Recommendation: lead performance proof on hero runners; fence lifestyle SKUs with explicit “everyday / commute” cues to protect running credibility.

2. **Convert contested quality into measurable POPs, then protect a design POD.** *(Evidence-backed)*  
   Perceived quality is relative (`01_05`). Prioritize closing **durability and fit** objections that block recommendation in enthusiast C2C; keep **design/CloudTec distinctiveness** as POD if Strength×Uniqueness remain high in the Brand Image Matrix. Survey comparative ratings vs Hoka/Nike/Asics before locking spend.

3. **Build loyalty loops at the model-adopter tier, not only awareness.** *(Partly evidence-backed; program design speculative)*  
   Loyalty value includes attracting new customers via recommenders and time to respond to threats (`01_06`). Run NPS **only among On users** (`03_08`); feed promoters with replacement-cycle / rotation education; address detractors on durability/value. Avoid assuming Cloud 5 visibility equals committed loyalty.
