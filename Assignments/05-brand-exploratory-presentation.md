## Overview / Purpose

Teams (to be selected randomly at the beginning of class) will present a succinct **10-minute** summary of the Brand Exploratory/Brand Strategy Report.

## Presentation Requirements

- Supported by PowerPoint slides (**20-slide maximum**)
- Should emphasize:
  - (a) what has been learned about the brand
  - (b) what is recommended for the brand going forward
- The PPT must include results for:
  - Awareness
  - Perceived quality
  - Brand associations
  - Brand loyalty
  - The Brand Identity–Brand Image Gap analysis described on the assignments page
- The **final slide** should be the strategy recommendations that flowed from the research
- Presentations will be strictly limited to **10 minutes**, with a short period following for Q&A and discussion
- Not all team members need to be involved in the presentation, but **all must be present**, at the front of the room, and prepared to discuss and answer questions
- Upload the PPT deck prior to class
