## Overview / Purpose

This tees up the Brand Audit assignment in which each team will study a different brand. In this ungraded assignment you need to form your team and work together to select the brand you wish to audit.

## Team Formation

1. Form your team by navigating to the **People** link on the course home page and then clicking on the **Project Groups** tab at the top.
2. Click to join a team (**minimum of five members, maximum of six**).
3. The instructor reserves the right to add a student to a group and for final approval of brands.
4. In the event that two or more teams choose the same brand, brands will be assigned to teams on a **first-come, first-serve** basis, so it is in your best interest to meet with your team, choose a captain, and select your brand early.
5. Feel free to e-mail the instructor your proposed brand in advance; feedback will be provided very quickly.
6. As soon as your brand has been approved for the audit, you should begin your research.
7. Although your brand selection is not due until October 15, you may submit your choice via **email (never Canvas messaging!)** any time up until then if you wish to get started earlier.
8. Once approved, the instructor will change your team number to your chosen brand.

## Planning Considerations

Be sure to satisfy yourselves that you can access enough information about the brand to complete the Brand Inventory portion of the Brand Audit. Give some consideration to:

- The sources of information you will use for the Brand Inventory
- The qualitative/quantitative techniques you plan to use to complete the Brand Exploratory
- Whether you can identify and access a sufficient number of current or prospective customers of the brand

## Free-Riding / Peer Evaluation

A note about the “free rider” problem: In the business world, you live and die by the results of your team as a whole. The instructor prefers to give a single grade to all members of a group, but understands there might be “outlier” behavior by particular group members.

After completing both the Brand Exploratory/Strategy Report, you will complete a peer evaluation assignment that rates the contribution of each team member. Please be fair when rating others. Since a significant portion of your grade depends on group work, the peer evaluations will be taken very seriously.

- If there appears to be a consensus that a group member did not pull his or her weight, the instructor will adjust an individual’s assignment grade down according to the peer evaluations.
- A non-contributing team member will receive zero peer evaluation points and will not be given an opportunity to make up the assignment.
